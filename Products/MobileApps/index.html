Mobile Ecosystem

MediVision Mobile Apps

Developed to streamline operations, save time, and boost your ROI. Whether you are a retailer, wholesaler, salesman, or patient, our mobile ecosystem connects everyone for seamless pharmacy management.

Our Specialized Applications

Four powerful mobile tools designed to eliminate communication gaps and empower every stakeholder in your medical business network.

🛒

Customer Connect

Empowers retailers to check live stock, view schemes, download old bills, and independently generate orders remotely without calling wholesalers.

💼

Salesman Connect

Allows salesmen to make bills on the wholesaler's server directly from the field. Track cash receipts, check live rates, and eliminate order bounces.

📊

MR Connect

Designed for Pharmacy Wholesalers. Medical Reps can view stock/sales statements, print purchase orders, and track party-wise sales from anywhere.

📱

Rx App (For Patients)

Patients can order directly from your chemist shop, send prescription photos, and track deliveries. Builds incredible customer loyalty.
